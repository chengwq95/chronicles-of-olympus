关于《奥林匹斯众神志》About Chronicles of Olympus

这是一座可切换中文或英文阅读的古希腊神话互动资料库，收录人物、谱系、史诗与经典故事。内容以常见传统和古代文献为基础；不同诗人、地区与时代的版本并不完全一致，部分关系和叙事细节因此可能有所差异。

An interactive Greek mythology archive available in either Chinese or English, covering figures, genealogies, epics and enduring stories. It draws on commonly recorded traditions and ancient sources; relationships and narrative details may vary across poets, regions and periods.
