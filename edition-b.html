Prologue

古希腊神话简介An Introduction to Greek Mythology

古希腊神话是古代希腊人讲述世界起源、诸神谱系、英雄功业与人类命运的故事总集。从原初的卡俄斯中诞生大地盖亚，到泰坦的兴衰、奥林匹斯众神的统治，再到赫拉克勒斯、奥德修斯等英雄的远征，这些故事塑造了西方文学、艺术与哲学两千余年的想象力。本站以人物为线索，带你系统地认识这一庞大而迷人的神话世界。

Greek mythology is the vast body of stories through which the ancient Greeks explained the origins of the world, the genealogies of the gods, the deeds of heroes and the fate of mortals. From primordial Chaos and Gaia the Earth, through the rise and fall of the Titans and the reign of the Olympians, to the voyages of Heracles and Odysseus, these tales have shaped Western literature, art and philosophy for over two millennia. This site takes its figures as your guide through that vast and fascinating world.
